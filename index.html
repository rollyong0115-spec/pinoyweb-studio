Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a domain name?

No. We can discuss free static hosting first. A custom domain is optional and may cost extra.

Can customers order directly on the website?

V1 packages can include Messenger-based order inquiries. Full checkout and automatic payment verification require additional development and separate pricing.

How long will my website take?

The delivery schedule depends on the package and whether your photos, text, and business details are ready. We agree on a timeline before starting.

Do you accept GCash or Maya?

Ask us about current payment options. We confirm payment instructions and terms directly before you pay.
